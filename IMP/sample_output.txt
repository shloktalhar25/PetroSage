│  6.  Practical recommendation – How to pick the “best” spot                                                         │
│                                                                                                                     │
│   1 Start with the upcoming licensing round (2026) and shortlist blocks that:                                       │
│      • Have positive geological indicators (seismic, basin studies).                                                │
│      • Are outside ESZs, wildlife sanctuaries, and CRZs, or where you can secure the necessary clearances for       │
│        exploratory work.                                                                                            │
│   2 Run a clearance feasibility check: map the block against MoEFCC’s ESZ notifications (e.g., the Kumbhalgarh      │
│     ESZ, Rajasthan – 243 sq km) and any protected‑area boundaries.                                                  │
│   3 Assess infrastructure and logistics: proximity to pipelines, roads, ports, and availability of water/land       │
│     rights (state‑level matters).                                                                                   │
│   4 Calculate the financial upside: royalty rates under the revised basin‑based framework (noted in the 2026        │
│     notification) and the stability clause that protects against future tax/royalty hikes.                          │
│   5 Proceed to bid for the block that scores highest on geological promise and clearance feasibility.               │
